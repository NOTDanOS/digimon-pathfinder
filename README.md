I made this as a little pet project for Digimon Cyber Sleuth. For context, I'm not really much of a stats guy, I care more about getting my favorite Digimon (granted, that's why half my party has stats all over the place).

Which got me thinking: how do I reach my favorite Mega-level digimon the fastest? That means, what's the minimum level I need to reach this Digimon properly? How many times must I de-digivolve to reach the appropriate ABI stat?
